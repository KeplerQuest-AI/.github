# KeplerQuest-AI

**KeplerQuest-AI** is an open technological framework that integrates **augmented reality (AR)** and **large language model (LLM)** technologies to support the teaching and learning of **Kepler’s laws of planetary motion** through interactive and inquiry-driven STEM learning environments.

This organization hosts the software artifacts associated with a research study accepted for presentation at the **2026 IEEE Global Engineering Education Conference (EDUCON)**. The repositories are released to enable **reproducibility, inspection, and reuse** of the underlying technology in other educational and research contexts.

---

## Repository Structure

The project is organized into two complementary and independent repositories, each addressing a distinct technological component of the framework:

### 🔹 Augmented Reality Client
**Repository:** `keplerquest_ar`  
**URL:** https://github.com/KeplerQuest-AI/keplerquest_ar  

Contains the Unity-based augmented reality client used to visualize and explore orbital mechanics associated with **Kepler’s laws**. This repository focuses exclusively on the AR technology stack and its configuration.

---

### 🔹 LLM-Based Chatbot Backend
**Repository:** `keplerquest_llm`  
**URL:** https://github.com/KeplerQuest-AI/keplerquest_llm  

Contains the backend infrastructure for the conversational agents that guide learners’ inquiry processes, including prompt handling, local LLM integration, and retrieval-augmented generation components.

---

## Scope and Reuse

This organization provides the **technological infrastructure** developed to support the learning of Kepler’s laws.  
Pedagogical design, learning activities, and experimental protocols are documented separately in the associated research publication.

Each repository includes documentation describing dependencies, configuration steps, and reproduction guidelines relevant to its respective component.
